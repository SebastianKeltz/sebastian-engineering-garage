Working principles

How I approach a hard problem.

Simple habits that keep complex builds understandable and improve the quality of every iteration.

01

Make it observable

A system is easier to debug when every important state can be measured, logged, or inspected.

02

Build the smallest proof

I isolate subsystems and validate one assumption at a time before increasing complexity.

03

Let the test change the design

The first model is a hypothesis. The bench, fit check, and ride test decide what survives.
