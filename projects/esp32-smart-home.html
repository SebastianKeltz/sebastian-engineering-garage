ESP32 Smart-Home Monitoring System

An ESP32-S3 platform for environmental sensing, serial diagnostics, and planned local automation.

The controller, firmware upload path, serial interface, flash, PSRAM, and BME280 sensor are verified. Additional sensors, physical fan control, automatic logic, and the dashboard remain under development.

Project Overview

Objective

Build a modular monitoring platform that reads environmental data, reports system state clearly, and provides a foundation for future motion, door, fan, and dashboard functions.

Development is staged so each sensor, output, and protection circuit can be checked independently before full-system integration.

Engineering Focus

Skills demonstrated

  • Embedded C++ and modular firmware development
  • I²C sensor integration and hardware debugging
  • GPIO planning and future PWM control
  • Power electronics and inductive-load protection
  • LTspice simulation and waveform comparison
  • System integration and technical documentation

System Architecture

Separate sensing, control, and power paths.

LTspice Comparison

Flyback protection limits the transient.

Without flyback protection ≈ 551 V
With flyback protection ≈ 12.4 V

The comparison shows why a switching path for an inductive load needs flyback protection. When the fan current is interrupted, stored magnetic energy can drive the MOSFET drain far above the 12 V supply unless the transient has a controlled path.

The physical MOSFET fan-driver and 12 V fan have not yet been assembled or tested.
