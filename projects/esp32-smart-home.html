In Progress

ESP32 Smart-Home Monitoring System

An ESP32-S3 platform for environmental sensing, serial diagnostics, and planned local automation.

The controller, firmware upload path, serial interface, flash, PSRAM, BME280 sensor, and GPIO 4 PIR motion input are verified. Additional sensors, physical fan control, automatic logic, and the dashboard remain under development.

Verified Work In Development Simulation Results

Project Overview

Objective

Build a modular monitoring platform that reads environmental and motion data, reports system state clearly, and provides a foundation for future door, fan, and dashboard functions.

Development is staged so each sensor, output, and protection circuit can be checked independently before full-system integration.

Engineering Focus

Skills demonstrated

  • I²C sensor integration and hardware debugging
  • GPIO planning and future PWM control
  • Power electronics and inductive-load protection
  • LTspice simulation and waveform comparison
  • System integration and technical documentation

LTspice Comparison

Flyback protection limits the transient.

Without flyback protection ≈ 550.73 V
With flyback protection ≈ 12.8 V

With the flyback diode, the simulated MOSFET drain voltage remained near the 12 V supply and reached a maximum of approximately 12.8 V. Without the diode, the fan's inductive switching spike reached approximately 550.73 V. The comparison demonstrates why the flyback diode is needed to protect the MOSFET.

The physical MOSFET fan-driver and 12 V fan have not yet been assembled or tested.
